# Nimbus Interstellar — Shipping Policy

- Standard orbit delivery: 3–5 standard days. Express (warp-assist): 24 hours.
- Refunds: damaged cargo is refundable within 30 days with a photo of the damage.
- Prohibited cargo: live krakens, unregistered AI cores, anything that hums ominously.
- Insurance: all containers are insured up to 50,000 credits.
- Lost cargo: open a ticket; our owls will find it.
