How it works

Learn it. Build it. Break it. Defend it.

Every concept goes through the same loop. We explain the mechanism, you build the control as code, you attack it yourself in your sandbox, and then you prove the defence holds.

  • Concept modules with real diagrams of how things work, not marketing slides.
  • 20+ labs with numbered steps, copy-paste code, checkpoints, hints, full solutions, and cleanup scripts.
  • CTF flags planted inside the lab environments. Do the lab properly and you'll find them.
  • Boss fights at the end of each day: open-ended scenarios that combine everything you learned.
  • XP, levels and badges tracked in your browser, so you can see how far you've come (and compete with your neighbour).

Fresh off the launch pad

What's new in AWS security, 2026, and where you'll use it

This course is built around the current platform, not 2019's. Here are the launches that change how you'd design AWS security today, each mapped to the day you'll use it.

Day 1
May 2026

Bigger SCPs

Up to 10 SCPs per root, OU or account, and 10,240 characters each. That's room for real guardrail libraries, written in the full IAM policy language.

Day 1–2
Oct–Nov 2026

Declarative policies for detection

Organizations declarative policies can now enforce GuardDuty and Security Hub CSPM enablement across every account and Region. Member accounts can't turn them off.

Day 1
Aug 2026

Identity Center, reworked

One-click multi-Region instances, optional account-access management (fewer service-linked roles), and the IAM account access manager for assigning roles to workforce users.

Day 2
May 2026

Richer attack sequences

GuardDuty Extended Threat Detection now attaches malicious-package, misconfiguration, reachability, sensitive-data and vulnerability indicators to attack sequences.

Day 2 & 4
Jul 2026

Security Hub for AI and multicloud

GuardDuty AI Protection for Bedrock and SageMaker, an organization-wide AI inventory, Azure posture coverage, and AI-powered investigations (preview).

Day 3
Oct 2026

Runtime Monitoring included

GuardDuty Runtime Monitoring is now part of the Security Hub Threat Analytics plan, which removes the usual cost objection to turning it on for EKS, ECS and EC2.

Day 4
Mar & Jun 2026

AgentCore Policy

Deterministic Cedar policies, enforced at the AgentCore Gateway outside the agent's code, decide which tool calls an agent may make. Since June they can also use Bedrock Guardrails.

Day 4
2025 → 2026

Post-quantum, for real

Hybrid ML-KEM TLS on AWS endpoints, ML-DSA signing keys in KMS and Private CA. The pre-standard Kyber draft is being retired from AWS endpoints in 2026, so check that your clients are ready.

Day 5
Mar 2026

AWS Security Agent

On-demand, context-aware penetration testing reached general availability, alongside the AWS DevOps Agent. Learn where AI agents help in a SOC, and where they need guardrails of their own.
